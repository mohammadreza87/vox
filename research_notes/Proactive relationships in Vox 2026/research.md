# Research evidence for proactive relationships in Vox, as of 19 October 2026

## How should an agent take initiative without becoming intrusive?

### Takeaway
Recent research supports treating initiative as an ongoing choice among speaking, waiting, and revisiting an intention. The strongest directly relevant studies remain small and context-specific; they support design principles, not a claim that a broadly reliable autonomous relationship engine has been demonstrated. [PITCH, CHI 2026](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790957); [Inner Thoughts, CHI 2025](https://stfang.me/assets/pdf/Inner_thoughts_CHI25.pdf)

### Cited Findings
- **Proactive AI: From Turns to Replannable Dialogue Timelines — Zijie Yang, submitted 4 October 2026; arXiv preprint, not verified peer-reviewed.** The full paper defines an event timeline, mutable pending actions, a periodic decision trigger, and silence as a valid outcome. Its two future-action types distinguish prepared messages from intentions to reconsider later. Every delivery is re-evaluated with current context; a new arrival can invalidate a pending model plan. A deterministic runtime handles scheduling and plan validation. The paper presents formal semantics, a research prototype, and an illustrative interaction, without an empirical user evaluation. Its optional bounded-silence mechanism eventually requires contact; that is a design proposal, not evidence that mandatory renewed contact benefits users. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05159v1); [publication record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05159)
- **Concrete prototype:** The associated Apache-2.0 repository contains a TypeScript timeline engine, model adapter, tests, and interactive demo. Its `reconsider` operation is especially close to the user's boss example: remember a purpose, then choose later whether and how to act. The repository describes itself as an exploration. I inspected its documentation, not its runtime or production reliability. [airalogy/proactive-ai](https://github.com/airalogy/proactive-ai)
- **Proactive Conversational Agents with Inner Thoughts — Liu et al., CHI 2025, peer-reviewed.** A formative study with 24 participants informed a five-stage process: trigger, retrieve, formulate candidate contributions, evaluate, participate. Ten human evaluators assessed 100 generated conversations; each rated five pairs, and the paper reports preference for this method in 82% of comparisons. A separate live Slack study involved 12 institutional participants in six pairs, each experiencing three 10-minute conversations with different initiative levels. This supports separating having something relevant to contribute from deciding to speak. It is evidence about brief multi-party text conversation, not months-long dyadic relationships or notification receptivity. [Author-hosted final paper](https://stfang.me/assets/pdf/Inner_thoughts_CHI25.pdf); [readable preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.00383v1)
- **“Having Lunch Now”: Understanding How Users Engage with a Proactive Agent for Daily Planning and Self-Reflection — Abbas et al., CHI 2026, peer-reviewed.** A 14-day PITCH deployment involved 12 graduate students, ages 22–35, using English Slack conversations with morning/evening check-ins at user-selected times. Five participants volunteered additional status updates; 109 of 336 conversations (32.4%) ended with an unanswered agent message. Qualitative failures included pursuing a productivity agenda despite distress, replying before multipart user answers were finished, and promising unavailable functionality. These observations support responsiveness to resistance and conversational completion. This was an exploratory coaching study in a small, technically familiar sample, not a randomized demonstration of companionship benefits. Use the 2026 final paper: some numerical analyses differ from the 2025 preprint. [Final paper](https://adnan-abbas.github.io/assets/pdf/PITCH_CHI_2026.pdf); [ACM record](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790957)

### Inferences
- **Vox design inference:** Store a follow-up intention such as “revisit the conflict with the boss when relevant,” rather than tomorrow's exact message. At reconsideration time, check whether the situation is resolved, a newer event matters more, or recent interaction suggests space. This applies the temporal prototype to the user's scenario; it is not an empirically established optimal policy. [Proactive AI](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05159v1)
- **Vox design inference:** Ordinary initiative does not require asking permission before each message. Make waiting, ending a conversation, changing subject, and canceling a pending follow-up first-class outcomes. Evaluate these alongside helpful initiation. These are proposed adaptations of the observed timing and rigidity failures. [PITCH](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790957)
- **Vox design inference:** Do not import the prototype's compulsory contact deadline as a default merely because it exists. Prefer an opportunity to decide again over a requirement to send. [Prototype's bounded-silence semantics](https://github.com/airalogy/proactive-ai)

### Gaps
- No source reviewed establishes a universal optimal frequency, delay, or “relationship score” for everyday companions.
- These studies do not establish that a model's self-reported motivation or confidence is calibrated evidence of user receptivity or emotion.
- Cross-cultural, multilingual, and months-long generalization remains unproven in these specific sources.

## What memory and evaluation support an evolving relationship?

### Takeaway
Remembering facts is necessary but insufficient: Vox must recognize updates, avoid false memories, and retrieve relevant history when nobody explicitly asks it to remember. Recent benchmarks offer complementary tests, not a single score proving relationship competence. [LongMemEval](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf); [LoCoMo-Conv](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03467v1)

### Cited Findings
- **LongMemEval: Benchmarking Chat Assistants on Long-Term Interactive Memory — Wu et al., ICLR 2025, peer-reviewed.** Five abilities are tested: extraction, multi-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates, and abstention. Its 500 curated questions use constructed user-assistant histories; standard settings include roughly 115,000 tokens or 500 sessions/~1.5 million tokens. Experiments separate indexing, retrieval, and reading, and demonstrate value in granular conversational records and time-aware retrieval. The benchmark explicitly tests changed user circumstances and false-premise questions. It evaluates memory-dependent answers, not unsolicited timing or user wellbeing. Avoid restating its historical “30% drop” as a finding about current 2026 models. [Final proceedings paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf); [official repository](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval)
- **Evaluating Very Long-Term Conversational Memory of LLM Agents (LoCoMo) — Maharana et al., ACL 2024, peer-reviewed.** The benchmark uses machine-generated conversations verified and edited by humans, averaging around 600 turns/16,000 tokens over up to 32 sessions. Tasks include question answering, event summarization, and multimodal dialogue generation. The official released benchmark contains ten conversations. It is useful for temporal and causal continuity, but its scale and generated nature do not warrant treating performance as representative of all real relationships. [ACL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.747/); [official dataset/code](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo)
- **When Users Don't Ask: Benchmarking Context-Driven Memory Retrieval in Conversational Agents (LoCoMo-Conv) — Wen-Yu Chang and Yun-Nung Chen, submitted 3 September 2026; arXiv preprint.** Rewrites LoCoMo questions into direct dialogue, implicit need, counterfactual, and composed-memory forms. Five memory systems are compared using a common embedder, answer model, and top-10 retrieval. In its setup, naive retrieval recall fell from 0.533 for direct dialogue to 0.312 for implicit queries. Memory retrieval and generated-response quality differed; abstracting a memory could help matching while losing useful detail. The paper also identifies “silent grounding”: context can improve a response without explicitly repeating the remembered fact. Synthetic rewrites, a fixed-history setup, and largely model-based judging limit generalization. This is not a live longitudinal user trial. [Full paper and Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03467v1); [official repository](https://github.com/MiuLab/LoCoMo-Conv/)

### Inferences
- **Vox design inference:** Separate source evidence, enduring preferences, time-bounded situations, and pending intentions. Include timestamps and provenance, allow corrections to supersede earlier claims, and distinguish something the user said from the agent's interpretation. That combination follows from update/temporal tests and the temporal-memory infrastructure below; it is a proposed schema, not a published universal standard. [LongMemEval](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf); [Graphiti](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti)
- **Vox evaluation inference:** Test natural scenarios: “It finally worked out” should close the relevant pending situation; “I'm dreading tomorrow” should retrieve a relevant recent event without “remember when” wording. Evaluate factual grounding, appropriate implicit use, corrections, and unjustified assumptions separately. Avoid rewarding repeated demonstrations that the agent remembers. [LoCoMo-Conv](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03467v1)

### Gaps
- These three benchmarks do not validate deletion propagation, tenant privacy, mood inference, acceptable notification frequency, relationship health, or wellbeing.
- A recommended decay period for tentative emotional observations cannot be derived from these papers. It needs task-specific evaluation; short-lived observations should not automatically become stable personality facts.
- No memory leaderboard ranking is recommended: differing models, retrieval limits, judges, and question subsets prevent casual score comparisons.

## How should the loop learn from outcomes, and what can we reuse?

### Takeaway
There is a sound experimental tradition for learning when an intervention adds value over doing nothing, plus practical infrastructure for changing memories and scheduled actions. None of those components alone proves that a personalized companion improves users' lives. [HeartSteps](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6401341/); [Action Centered Contextual Bandits](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/4fa177df22864518b2d7818d4db5db2d-Abstract.html)

### Cited Findings
- **Efficacy of Contextually Tailored Suggestions for Physical Activity: A Micro-randomized Optimization Trial of HeartSteps — Klasnja et al., Annals of Behavioral Medicine, online 2018/issue 2019, peer-reviewed.** A six-week trial recruited 44 adults; 37 remained in the analyzed sample. At up to five daily decision points, it randomized whether to deliver a context-tailored walking/antisedentary suggestion or no suggestion. Walking suggestions increased subsequent 30-minute steps by 24% on average (p=.02), with effects declining over the study. The combined suggestion estimate was 14% with p=.06; it should not be reported as conventionally statistically significant. Participants were sedentary adults around Ann Arbor, not companion-app users. Its relevance is causal experimental design and habituation, not evidence that affectionate messages improve wellbeing. [Full trial report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6401341/)
- **Action Centered Contextual Bandits — Greenewald et al., NeurIPS 2017, peer-reviewed foundational methods.** Models the incremental effect of an action relative to the baseline/no-action option, allowing the baseline to be complex and nonstationary while imposing structure on treatment effects. It provides theoretical results and experiments informed by HeartSteps data; it is not an end-to-end prospective trial of a relationship chatbot. Useful concept: whether a person responds after a message differs from whether sending that message improved the outcome compared with silence. [Official abstract](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/4fa177df22864518b2d7818d4db5db2d-Abstract.html); [full paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/4fa177df22864518b2d7818d4db5db2d-Paper.pdf)
- **Graphiti — current official open-source repository, checked 19 October 2026.** Implements temporal context graphs with validity windows, provenance back to episodes, incremental updates, and semantic/keyword/graph retrieval. Old facts can be invalidated while preserving history. It is suitable infrastructure to explore for “used to work here; now works there” and source-backed memory. The repository explicitly distinguishes the framework from managed Zep: application/user management and operating the surrounding system remain developer responsibilities. It does not supply a validated relationship policy or prove its extracted facts are correct. [Official repository](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti)
- **Letta — current official repositories, checked 19 October 2026.** The original `letta-ai/letta` now points active development to `letta-ai/letta-code`; its old V1 API server is on an archive branch. Letta Code documents persistent agent memory, context rewriting, periodic memory processing, scheduled/heartbeat work, and Telegram/Slack/Discord integrations. These are relevant implementation examples for continuing state across sessions. “Learning” here includes rewriting memory and prompts; the documentation does not establish reliable autonomous relationship adaptation or clinical benefit. Its vendor phrasing about agents being like people should be treated as positioning. [Current harness](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code); [migration/current-source notice](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta)

### Inferences
- **Vox learning inference:** Use a fast loop for updating facts, unresolved situations, and observed preferences, and a slower evaluated loop for changing routing/timing policy. First record candidate action, context, policy version, decision, and observed outcome; a model score is not an outcome. Start with interpretable rules and offline evaluation before adaptive bandit exploration. [LongMemEval](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d813d324dbf0598bbdc9c8e79740ed01-Paper-Conference.pdf); [Action Centered Contextual Bandits](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/4fa177df22864518b2d7818d4db5db2d-Abstract.html)
- **Vox experimental inference:** On eligible low-stakes opportunities, compare sending with waiting/no contact, and measure user-reported appropriateness/helpfulness, corrections, unwanted-message signals, and task-relevant outcomes. Treat reply rate as a behavioral observation, not a wellbeing measure. The HeartSteps result illustrates why effects should be tracked over time rather than assuming an initial response persists. [HeartSteps](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6401341/)
- **Implementation choice inference:** Borrow the reconsiderable-intention concept from Proactive AI and temporal provenance from Graphiti, or evaluate Letta as a broader stateful harness. These are options to prototype; this research did not benchmark operational fit with Vox or establish a best library. [Proactive AI](https://github.com/airalogy/proactive-ai); [Graphiti](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti); [Letta Code](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code)

### Gaps
- No reviewed source establishes a production-proven, end-to-end companion that combines culturally robust mood interpretation, reliable unsolicited timing, continual memory correction, user-benefit optimization, and autonomous app development.
- No recommended universal numerical weights, outreach caps, quiet-hour assumptions, or policy-update cadence are justified by this literature alone.
- Code-generating product development is outside these studies. Evidence that memory and routing improve does not demonstrate that an agent can safely redesign and release the application from private conversation feedback.
