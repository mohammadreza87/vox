# Real products for proactive ongoing AI relationships

Research cutoff and access date: 19 October 2026. All listed sources were opened, not merely read through search snippets. This is documentary research, not hands-on product testing. Confidence below distinguishes confidence in the published capability from confidence in its actual quality. Nine primary sources substantiate five product examples; four are relationship companions and the fifth is an adjacent assistant precedent.

## Which products most closely implement the intended Vox relationship loop?

### Takeaway

Kindroid is the closest documented current feature comparison; Nomi illustrates restraint after silence; ElliQ supplies an explicit orchestration architecture; Replika documents continuity but reveals fewer timing mechanics. These are vendor-described capabilities, not independent proof that any system achieves human-quality judgment.

### Cited Findings

**1. Kindroid — closest current analogue.**

- Official help was updated **18 October 2026**. Away proactive actions analyze conversation patterns and choose context-dependent text, voice message, selfie, or call. Paid subscribers can enable up to ten companions; the feature is on by default for newly created companions when there are fewer than three. Directives influence behavior and calls have quiet hours. It pauses after three unanswered contacts, resuming on interaction. A resumed chat cancels and replans a pending action. Learned Context automatically maintains three notes: relationship growth, important facts, and ongoing situations; favoriting influences future updates. Recent emoji reactions are visible to the AI. This combines initiation, pacing, evolving memory, and feedback without approval before every message. Confidence: **high for current documented behavior; unmeasured quality**. — [Kindroid: Chat features and tools](https://kindroid.ai/v2/docs/chat-features-and-tools/)

**2. Nomi — concrete backoff and relationship adaptation.**

- Its **9 September 2024** official guide describes proactive messaging after inactivity, with approximate initiation windows of one hour, three hours, one day, or four days. The first three settings increase spacing after no response; the last sends only once until a reply. It documents local quiet hours from 10pm–8am, per-companion controls, free-account availability, and notification previews that omit message content. Disabling device notifications need not disable in-app proactive messages. This older, currently published guide says away group messages and proactive calls/voice messages are unsupported; those limitations should not be treated as freshly verified UI behavior. Confidence: **high for the published design; medium for unchanged implementation details in 2026**. — [Nomi proactive messaging guide](https://nomi.ai/nomi-knowledge/proactive-messaging-when-your-nomi-messages-you-first/)
- Nomi's **12 December 2024** Identity Core announcement describes a continuously revised memory mechanism incorporating significant facts, shared experiences, preferences, and explicit or implicit feedback, alongside other memory layers. It claims stable personality with gradual adaptation. Anthropomorphic descriptions of identity are marketing language, not evidence of consciousness or disclosure of model-training mechanics. Confidence: **high that this was a released vendor-described mechanism; unknown accuracy or internal implementation**. — [Nomi Identity Core](https://nomi.ai/updates/introducing-the-nomi-identity-core-fostering-dynamic-and-authentic-identities/)

**3. ElliQ — strongest explicit relationship orchestration precedent.**

- In its **9 January 2024** ElliQ 3 announcement, Intuition Robotics describes a Relationship Orchestration Engine choosing among actions, scripted dialogue, and generative dialogue in real time. Spoken information and non-spoken choices are classified, remembered, and reused in the profile/context for relevant follow-ups. It describes dynamic context switching and preserved character, empathy, humor, and curiosity. This is an architecture description from the vendor, not disclosed source code or a comparative evaluation. Confidence: **high for the architecture claim; internal details unverified**. — [New Year, New ElliQ](https://blog.elliq.com/elliq3)
- The **undated current FAQ**, accessed 19 October 2026, describes available English-language service within the United States, designed for adults 60+ who spend substantial time alone. It initiates check-ins when users are available; nearby-presence sensing informs timing, and nighttime sleep mode reduces disturbance. After initiating, it listens briefly and stops if unanswered. Idle operation is wake-word listening rather than background conversation recording. Initial onboarding personalizes the experience, and sharing updates with caregivers is separately enabled and directed. Confidence: **high for current stated availability and controls; no direct device verification**. — [ElliQ FAQ](https://elliq.com/pages/faqs)

**4. Replika — established example with less transparent scheduling.**

- Its **undated current notification guide**, accessed 19 October 2026, explicitly describes messages following up on previous conversations and checking how the user feels. Enabling notifications leads to outreach throughout the day; it does not describe requiring approval for each check-in. It does not disclose precise timing, backoff, plan eligibility, or the distinction between scripted and generated notifications. Confidence: **high for feature description, low for undisclosed scheduling mechanics**. — [Replika notification guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360027515872-How-do-I-set-up-my-app-s-notifications)
- Its **undated current memory guide** describes visible memories plus deeper conversation-pattern memory, automatic personalization, manual additions, and reinforcement through affirming or upvoting accurate recall. The statement about memory working “locally within your experience” should not be interpreted as an on-device computing claim; the article does not establish that. Confidence: **high for documented product behavior, unspecified implementation**. — [Replika memory guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37208679176077-How-does-Replika-s-memory-work)

### Inferences

- For Vox, the transferable pattern is evolving context plus event/time-based reconsideration plus outreach restraint. This is an architectural interpretation of the examples above, not an industry-certified standard.
- Users can experience natural initiative while still having product-level settings and an immediate ability to stop. A mode setting is different from repeated conversational permission requests.
- A missed reply should change pacing without being interpreted as proof of rejection, loneliness, or a specific emotion.

### Gaps

- The reviewed vendor pages do not provide calibrated accuracy for emotional interpretation, cross-cultural follow-up appropriateness, false-memory rates, or long-term comparative outcomes.
- Kindroid does not disclose how it optimizes timing, validates memory updates, or prevents subtle emotional-pressure behavior. Its displayed thought bubbles are a product feature, not independently verifiable reasoning traces.
- No reviewed source proves that voice emotion recognition feeds an accurate persistent relationship-state estimator. Voice support alone does not establish emotion understanding.
- None of these pages documents autonomous application feature development from relationship data. That is a distinct Vox workstream, not a demonstrated implication of companion memory.

## Is ChatGPT Pulse a current example in October 2026?

### Takeaway

Pulse is a useful historical design precedent, but it must not be presented as a currently available standalone feature. OpenAI announced its sunset in June 2026, moving proactive updates into scheduled tasks.

### Cited Findings

- **25 September 2025:** Pulse launched as a Pro mobile preview that researched overnight and delivered daily personalized cards based on history, memory, feedback, and optional connected apps. It used thumbs feedback and curation; OpenAI acknowledged stale suggestions such as updates about already-completed projects. The release framed bounded daily delivery as preferable to endless consumption. Confidence: **high for historical launch and stated design; not current availability**. — [Introducing ChatGPT Pulse](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-pulse/)
- **17 June 2026:** English release notes state that Pulse was being sunset, with Pro access continuing for fourteen more days, as proactive updates moved into scheduled tasks. The same section describes specific times or broad delivery windows, meaningful-change-only monitoring notifications, pause/edit/delete controls, no more than hourly execution, and possible automatic pausing after inactivity. Confidence: **high for explicit current product transition; no user-account testing**. — [ChatGPT release notes, June 17, 2026](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)

### Inferences

- Pulse illustrates memory-to-recommendation-to-feedback, while scheduled tasks illustrate durable timing and suppression controls. Neither source proves a spontaneous emotionally adaptive relationship engine.
- Its sunset is not proof that proactivity failed; the official source says functionality moved and does not establish a causal product-performance conclusion.

### Gaps

- No source reviewed supplies a controlled evaluation of Pulse versus scheduled tasks for relationship quality or emotional appropriateness.

## What should Vox reuse, and how strong is the evidence?

### Takeaway

The evidence establishes that the proposed interaction pattern is already productized in several forms. It does not establish a single best implementation or validate “superintelligent” emotional inference.

### Cited Findings

- Sources above cover current help documents and dated vendor announcements. Each direct example specifies autonomous outreach plus broader user controls; none requires approval before every ordinary conversational follow-up. The strongest directly inspectable mechanisms are Kindroid's dynamic memory/replanning and Nomi's nonresponse pacing. — [Kindroid](https://kindroid.ai/v2/docs/chat-features-and-tools/); [Nomi](https://nomi.ai/nomi-knowledge/proactive-messaging-when-your-nomi-messages-you-first/)

### Inferences

- Suggested Vox design: keep durable facts, temporary interpretations, unresolved situations, and communication preferences separate; reconsider each planned follow-up against fresh context before sending; support cancel/supersede; treat quiet/no-action as valid decisions; use response patterns conservatively; maintain simple user corrections.
- Distinguish content relevance, timing suitability, delivery permission, and message generation. They are separate decisions even if the user experiences one fluid relationship.
- Useful evaluation cases: the boss issue resolves before the planned follow-up; a user ignores three messages; a user explicitly dislikes advice; cultural style differs from language stereotypes; voice sounds tired but the user says they feel happy; a calendar changes; the user asks to forget a fact.
- Avoid using reply rate alone as success: it cannot distinguish welcome engagement from obligation or emotional pressure. Suggested measures include unwanted-outreach reports, correct closure of situations, accurate recall/corrections, user-rated usefulness, and healthy pacing.

### Gaps

- These are design recommendations derived from documented products; they require Vox-specific evaluation. No hands-on accounts, purchases, signups, provider outreach, or product changes were performed.
- Public vendor claims should be paired with independently assessed research before making efficacy or wellbeing claims in the final report. Academic evidence is outside this product-survey assignment and should come from the parallel research workstream.
